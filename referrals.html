Referral Program

Referral Program Terms

Refer a client and earn 10% of their first project fee, paid after the project is complete.

Effective December 2025 · Last updated October 2, 2026

Program overview

The APOM Research Referral Program rewards attorneys, advocates, and professionals who connect qualified clients with our digital forensics and OSINT investigation services.

Eligibility

Who can participate

  • Attorneys, family law attorneys, and legal professionals
  • Victim advocates and nonprofit organizations
  • Professional investigators and consultants
  • Individuals who know someone needing investigation services

Referral fee

10% of the first project fee, paid after project completion and final payment from the client.

Payment timeline

Referral fees are paid within 30 days of receiving final payment from the referred client.

Payment methods

  • Wire transfer (preferred; banking details required)
  • Zelle (subject to platform limits; amounts over $2,000 may require multiple payments)

Exclusions and limitations

What does not qualify

  • Self-referrals
  • Prospects already in contact with APOM Research
  • Clients referred by multiple sources (the first referrer receives the fee)
  • Ongoing work or repeat projects (initial project only)

One fee per client

The referral fee applies to the first project only. Subsequent projects or ongoing retainer work do not generate additional fees.

New clients only

The referred party must be a new client who has not previously contacted APOM Research or been referred by another source.

How to submit a referral

Email hello@apomarchives.com with the subject line Client Referral. Include your name and contact information, the referred client's name and contact information, a one- or two-sentence case description, how you know the client, and your preferred payment method.

Tracking

APOM Research will confirm receipt within 48 hours, notify you when the client schedules a consultation, update you when a contract is signed, and process payment within 30 days of final client payment.

Confidentiality

All referral information remains confidential. We will not disclose that you referred the client without your explicit permission.

Tax and legal

Tax reporting

Referral fees totaling $600 or more in a calendar year will be reported to the IRS on Form 1099-NEC. You will receive a copy by January 31 of the following year.

Independent contractor status

Referral participants are independent contractors, not employees of APOM Research, and are responsible for their own tax obligations on referral income.

No guarantee of payment

Referral fees are contingent on the client signing a contract, the project being completed, and final payment being received. If a client does not proceed, cancels mid-project, or fails to pay, no referral fee is owed.

Program changes

APOM Research may modify fee percentages or terms, or discontinue the program, with 30 days' written notice to active participants. Referrals submitted before a change takes effect are honored under the original terms.

Ethical standards

  • Make honest, accurate representations about APOM Research services
  • Make no guarantees about investigation outcomes
  • Disclose the referral relationship where professional ethics rules require it
  • Comply with all applicable bar rules, professional codes, and laws

Attorneys must ensure any referral arrangement complies with their jurisdiction's ethics rules on client referrals, fee sharing, and conflicts of interest.

Questions

Email hello@apomarchives.com with the subject line Referral Program Inquiry.
