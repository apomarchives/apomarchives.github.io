Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

For an audience being surveilled, this page is a conversion asset. It should read like it was written by someone who knows what data exposure costs, because it was.

What I collect

  • Your name and email address, through Flodesk, when you join the waitlist or purchase
  • Payment records, processed by Stripe. I never see or store your card number
  • Your Telegram handle, when you join the program channel
  • Nothing else. No analytics pixels beyond what the platforms themselves run, no tracking I add, no data enrichment, no appending

What I do with it

  • Deliver the program you bought and the emails you signed up for
  • Nothing else. I do not sell, rent, share, or trade your information with anyone, for any reason, ever

Deliberate data minimization

I collect the minimum required to deliver what you paid for, on purpose. What I don't hold can't be exposed, breached, or demanded. Do not send me case details, evidence, documents, or anything about your legal matter through email, direct message, or Telegram. I am not able to hold them confidentially and the program doesn't require them.

Payment discretion

The charge on your statement does not name this program.

Telegram

The program runs on a broadcast channel. Comments are off. Other subscribers cannot see who is in the channel or interact with you. Your Telegram display name is visible to me as the channel owner. Set your Telegram privacy settings before joining; the Day 1 checklist covers which ones.

Retention and deletion

Email me from your purchase address to have your information deleted; anything not legally required for tax and payment records gets removed.

Emails

Every email has a working unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing from marketing does not cut off program delivery emails you paid for.
