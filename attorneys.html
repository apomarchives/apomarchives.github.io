Why It Matters

Why This Matters For Your Case

Digital evidence is increasingly central to family law, IP litigation, and civil litigation, and increasingly challenged.

My documentation protocols produce evidence packages that are organized, authenticated, and ready for direct use.

Common vulnerabilities I eliminate
Screenshots without metadata (easily challenged as fabricated)
Timestamps without verification (capture time vs. post time)
Evidence without chain of custody (gaps are ammunition for opposing counsel)
Scattered documentation that overwhelms judges and loses impact
Opposing evidence that goes unchallenged because no one reviewed it technically
Standards

Admissibility Standards I Build To

Every evidence package is constructed to meet the authentication requirements courts actually apply, in California and federal court.

Cal. Evid. Code §§1400–1402
Authentication of writings, including electronic records and messages
Cal. Evid. Code §1552
Presumption of accuracy for computer printouts, and what it takes to rebut it
FRE 901(b)(9)
Documented collection methodology with hash verification
FRE 902(13–14)
Machine-generated records with hash certification
Chain of Custody
Documented handling from collection through court submission with no evidentiary gaps
Metadata Integrity
