Keep the originals of anything you might need as evidence.

The hidden details in a photo can prove when and where something happened. This tool makes a new, clean copy for sharing and leaves your original file exactly as it was. Keep that original somewhere safe.

Where this matters most

  • Email attachments and AirDrop. The full file goes, hidden details and all.
  • Shared cloud links. Google Drive, Dropbox, and iCloud links share the original file.
  • Listings and forums. Marketplace posts, rental listings, school and parent groups.
  • Messages sent as files. Many apps strip some of this when you post. You can't check what they keep, so clean it first.

What this can't do

  • It can't change what's in the picture. A street sign, a license plate, a school logo, the view from your window, or a reflection can still show where you are.
  • Some dashcams and action cameras record a running GPS log inside the video itself. If a file has one, you'll see a warning.
  • Uploading still shows your connection. A clean file hides what's inside it. The site you upload to can still see the internet connection you used.
