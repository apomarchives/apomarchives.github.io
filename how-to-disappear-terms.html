Education only

The program teaches lawful privacy and relocation practices for educational purposes. It is not legal advice, investigative services, safety planning, or emergency services. It does not execute any action on your behalf. Decisions about your situation are yours, made with your own professional advisors.

No professional relationship, no privilege, no confidentiality

Purchasing this program does not create an attorney-client relationship, an investigator-client relationship, or any professional engagement between us. No privilege attaches to anything you send me. Communications you send through email, direct message, or any channel are not legally confidential and could be subject to legal process. This is one more reason the program is built so you never need to send me case details, and you shouldn't.

No outcome warranty

Privacy work is maintenance against systems that change. I teach the current lawful state of those systems and update the program between cohorts. I cannot guarantee any specific result, including that any person will be unable to locate you.

Eligibility

This program is for women who have already left, and for mothers who are legally free to relocate: sole custody or documented legal permission. Women without children have no relocation restriction. If you have an active custody matter, relocation decisions run through your attorney. Your checkout attestation confirms this.

Built for the United States

Payment plans

Your materials

Program audio and documents are for your personal use. Sharing channel access or distributing materials ends your access without refund. I built the price so women wouldn't need to share it.

Not emergency services
