Security and privacy

How it protects her.

Everything she saves is encrypted on her phone with a key held in the phone's secure keychain. Nothing is readable without her phone unlocked.
Her log and files are kept out of iCloud and Google backups, so a shared or compromised cloud account can't carry them anywhere.
There are no accounts, no logins and no server holding her evidence. We can't see it, hand it over or lose it.
Every file she attaches is sealed with a SHA-256 hash the moment it's saved, so an attorney or examiner can prove it hasn't changed since.
One feature reaches our server: Analyze My Evidence sends the text of her entries for pattern analysis, and we don't keep it. She chooses when to run it. Details are in the privacy policy.
Discreet mode disguises the app as an ordinary one if he picks up her phone, and she can erase everything from the device in Settings.
The terms, in plain words
10% of every month a client you send stays subscribed, for as long as she stays. Not a one-time bounty.
Your own link. Apple and Google attribute downloads and proceeds to it, with a 24 to 72 hour lag, and the links slightly undercount. So I review the numbers by hand every month and err in your favor.
Paid monthly by Wise. No minimum, no waiting for a threshold.
On Android, the next update reads your link automatically, so every client you send counts exactly. On iPhone, Apple's reports run slightly low, so I check them by hand every month.
No tracking code, no pixel, nothing added to your site or your clients' phones.

About StayGone

StayGone is a safety and evidence app for women who've left an abusive partner and are still being watched through their phones, accounts, cars and kids' devices. It walks her through finding every way he still has access, logs each incident with a tamper-evident seal, exports a court-ready record for her attorney or a police report, checks his evidence for signs of editing, and disguises itself as an ordinary app if he picks up her phone. Everything stays on her phone. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no copy anywhere else. Full access is $29.99 a month, and free for survivors through partner programs.

About the founder

Kyērstin O'Neal is a digital forensics investigator and the founder of APOM Research, where she documents technology-facilitated abuse for attorneys and survivors. Her cybersecurity background includes the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency and Sandia National Laboratories. She built StayGone on privacy and cybersecurity principles, and as a survivor of coercive control, post-separation abuse and family court injustice. It's built by a survivor, for survivors. She's the author of a book on digital evidence for attorneys and is writing its companion for survivors.
