About StayGone

StayGone is a safety and evidence app for women who've left an abusive partner and are still being watched through their phones, accounts, cars and kids' devices. It walks her through finding every way he still has access, logs each incident with a tamper-evident seal, exports a court-ready record for her attorney or a police report, checks his evidence for signs of editing, and hides behind a working water tracker if he picks up her phone. Everything stays on her phone. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no copy anywhere else. Full access is $29.99 a month, and free for survivors through partner programs.

About the founder

Kyērstin O'Neal is a digital forensics investigator and the founder of APOM Research, where she documents technology-facilitated abuse for attorneys and survivors. Her cybersecurity background includes the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency and Sandia National Laboratories. She wrote StayGone after clawing her way through family court as a mother being tracked and surveilled, and built it to be the tool she didn't have. She's the author of a book on digital evidence for attorneys and is writing its companion for survivors.
