About the app
FOR SHELTERS, COALITIONS AND ADVOCATES

Free for the survivors you work with.

StayGone is for women who've left, or are leaving, and are still being watched. Watch the two-minute walkthrough, then read how a client gets full access at no cost. If you have questions after that, book twenty minutes.

Two-minute walkthrough

The app, exactly as your clients would see it.

A guided safety audit, a sealed evidence log (every file SHA-256 hashed), court-ready exports, a check on his evidence, and a way to disguise the app as an ordinary one if he picks up her phone. Her evidence stays on her phone. There are no accounts and we never hold a copy.

Captioned, no sound needed. Forward the link to anyone on your team.

Free access

Nothing on a card, nothing on a statement.

For survivors coming through a shelter, coalition or advocacy program, full access is free, for life. There's no card on file, no billing, nothing in her inbox and nothing on a bank statement he could see.

The Support ID is a random string that identifies her install and nothing else. It carries no name, no email and no phone number. It usually takes me under a day to turn it on.

How a client gets it
1

She installs StayGone from the App Store or Google Play, and on the subscription screen, before paying anything, taps Copy on her Support ID.

2

She sends it to you, and you send it to hello@apomarchives.com.

3

I unlock full access on her phone. It stays unlocked through every update.
