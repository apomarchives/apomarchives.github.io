Questions

What you might be wondering.

Is StayGone safe to have on my phone?
Yes. Discreet Mode disguises the app as a hydration tracker, every screen has a quick-exit, and your entries are stored on your device, encrypted where your device supports it. Your content stays on your phone unless you choose to send something for analysis. See “How does Analyze My Evidence work?” below.
How does Analyze My Evidence work?
Analyze My Evidence is optional, and it only runs when you tap Analyze. When you do, the incident text and any files you attach are sent to our analysis service, which uses Anthropic’s language models to produce your analysis. Our own servers don’t store that evidence. Anthropic processes it to generate your result and deletes it within 30 days, except where content is flagged by its safety systems or retention is legally required, and does not use it to train its models. If you’d rather not send a particular piece of evidence, you don’t have to. You can still log it, hash it, and keep it on your device. Check His Evidence works differently: it scans files entirely on your phone and never transmits them.
Will he be able to tell I’m using it?
No. The audit gives you steps in an order designed so nothing alerts him before you’re ready, and the app can be hidden in plain sight whenever he’s near.
Does this replace a lawyer?
No. StayGone is an educational and documentation tool - it does not provide legal advice and does not create an attorney-client relationship. It helps you walk into your attorney’s office organized and informed.
